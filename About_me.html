About Me

My interest in 3D design began with playing tabletop, analog and video games in central Kentucky. Through this, I developed an appreciation for character design. Northern Kentucky University’s 3D & VFX program allowed me to explore multiple programs, fueling my passion for digital sculpting. As a digital sculptor, I strive to create characters that immerse the viewer in different worlds, drawing inspiration from my interests in fitness and outdoor exploration.

Artist Statement

As a digital sculptor, I strive to capture the world and settings characters inhabit. My work focuses on the advancements within 3D printing for the tabletop and figurine scene, while demonstrating the practical application of character design through professional rendering in ZBrush and Adobe Substance Painter.
