About Me

My interest in 3D design began with playing tabletop, analog and video games in central Kentucky, It is through this I developed a appreciation for the visual design of characters . Northern Kentucky University and it’s 3D & VFX program allowed me to explore multiple programs, which allowed my passion for digital sculpting to truly soar. As a digital sculptor, I strive to create characters that embody different worlds and that immerse the viewer. I hope to take my interests such as fitness and outdoors exploration as a way to help fuel my work.

Artist Statement

As a digital sculptor, I strive to create characters that perfectly capture the world/settings they are in. My work is more focused on showing the growing strides within 3D printing for the tabletop/figurine scene. However, I still wish to show the practical application of character design and sculpting in a digital environment through rendering in both Zbrush or adobe substance painter. I hope to provide the hobbyists with models to play their games or gaming studios for their characters.
